# Privacy Policy — Personal Oura Health Bot

Last updated: October 14, 2026

## Overview

Personal Oura Health Bot is a personal project that helps its owner review health and wellness data from an Oura Ring and organize summaries in Obsidian.

## Data collected

Depending on the features enabled, the project may process Oura data such as sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, workouts, and other data authorized by the account owner. The owner may also enter nutrition, exercise, and personal notes manually.

## How data is used

Data is used only to support personal health tracking, trend reviews, and weekly summaries.

## Storage and sharing

The project is designed to store health records locally on the owner's computer, including in a local database and an Obsidian vault. The project does not intentionally publish personal health records in this public repository.

If an optional AI service is configured, selected information may be sent to that service to generate summaries. The owner should review the AI provider's privacy practices before enabling that feature.

Oura data is accessed through the Oura API with the account owner's authorization and is subject to Oura's applicable terms and policies.

## Security and retention

The owner is responsible for securing their computer, API credentials, local database, backups, and any third-party services they choose to use. Data may be deleted from local storage by the owner.

## Contact

For questions about this personal project, contact the repository owner through the GitHub profile associated with this repository.
